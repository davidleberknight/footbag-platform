**What sanctioning gives your event**
- It's listed on footbag.org as IFPA-sanctioned, and you may use the IFPA logo, available to download from this page, in your publicity.
  Download the IFPA logo: [PDF, for print](/img/ifpa-logo-print.pdf) or [PNG, high resolution](/img/ifpa-logo-large.png).
- It can take entry fees online through footbag.org, if you choose.
- Its results are official IFPA results and count toward world rankings.
- Sponsors and donors can give to IFPA designated for your event.

**Who can apply.** The organizer must be an IFPA Organizer Member (Tier 2) or hold a lifetime or honorary membership (Tier 3). Apply on footbag.org when you create your event. An administrator reviews each request, and the IFPA Sanctioning Director is notified and may contact you first. If a request is turned down, you'll get the reason and can revise and resubmit. A World Footbag Championships awarded by the IFPA Board is automatically sanctioned.

**Divisions.** Every category a sanctioned tournament offers, for example Singles Net or Freestyle Routines, must include a Women's division and an Intermediate division alongside Open. If too few players enter at the time of the tournament, divisions may be combined.

**Prize money.** In any division that offers prize money, players must be able to enter as an Amateur. Amateurs take no prize money but earn the same placing and ranking points. This keeps prize events open to amateur players, in line with IFPA's purpose of fostering amateur competition. An event without prize money needs no separate Amateur option, since every player competes as an amateur.

**Players.** Anyone can enter a sanctioned event; IFPA membership is not required. Players who register online need a footbag.org account, which is free. Players who register online give their own details, so you don't need to collect them.

**What you agree to.** By requesting sanctioning, you agree that:
1. The event follows these guidelines.
2. Competition uses the formats and judging systems in the Official Rules of Footbag Sports, or you state on your request where the event differs.
3. If you use an alternative judging system, you tell all players about it before competition begins.
4. You meet the after-event requirements below.

**After your event.** Within 20 days of the event's last day:
- Upload the complete results for every competition on footbag.org. If they aren't uploaded by then, the event shows "sanctioning requirements not met" until they are.
- Send a brief summary of the event, a paragraph or two on its highlights, to the IFPA announce list (`announce@footbag.org`). Use the Send Announcement form on footbag.org, which emails it to everyone on the list.

**Permits and insurance.** The organizer is responsible for any permits, facility agreements and insurance the venue or local law requires. IFPA does not provide insurance for sanctioned events.

**Entry fees.** You may collect entry fees online through footbag.org, or collect them yourself. Fees paid on footbag.org are paid out to the organizers by the IFPA Treasurer after the event, or earlier by arrangement with the Treasurer. The Treasurer arranges any refunds of them.

**Sponsorship and donations for your event.** Sponsors and donors may give to IFPA and designate the gift for your event, and IFPA forwards the money to you. They can donate online at footbag.org or pay IFPA directly, with checks made out to IFPA. Either way, the giver must name your event, in the donation comment online or in the check memo, for the gift to be earmarked for it. Gifts may be tax-deductible only for the amount above the value of anything the giver receives in return; the giver decides deductibility. Online donors get their receipt automatically. For gifts made any other way, send the Treasurer a list of who gave what, with the value of any product or service provided in return, so IFPA can send receipts.

**Publicity.** Promote your event on footbag.org, on social media, and through your local footbag community. After the event, share your results and highlights on social media too.

**Questions:** sanctioning@footbag.org
